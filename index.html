Everything a roster needs

From the first signed artist to the last encore, the tools sit side by side instead of in six different spreadsheets.

  • Artist profiles

    Photo, biography, social links, discography and bookings on one page for every artist on your roster.

  • Song catalogue

    Track titles, albums, genres, durations, release dates and cover art, and hear a short preview with one click.

  • Analytics dashboard

    Artists by genre, songs per release year, top artists and upcoming gigs, scoped to what each person may see.

  • Gigs and bookings

    A month calendar with status markers keeps holds, confirmed dates and completed shows in view.

  • Playlists and favorites

    Group songs into ordered playlists and star the artists and tracks you come back to most.

  • Tables that work

    Sort, filter, search, paginate, choose columns, act on many rows at once, and import or export CSV.

The right access for every person

Three roles, enforced on every screen and every request.

  • Admin

    Full control: users and roles, every artist and song, the complete audit trail.

  • Artist manager

    Runs the roster: creates and edits artists, songs and gigs, imports data, curates playlists.

  • User

    Read-only access to their own artists and songs, plus personal playlists and favorites.
